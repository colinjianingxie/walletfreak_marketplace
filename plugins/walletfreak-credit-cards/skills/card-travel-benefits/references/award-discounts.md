# Airline cardmember awards and cash charges

Official terms checked 2026-10-17. These examples identify rules to recheck, not permanent discounts or verified inventory. Compare the actual signed-in quote for the exact eligible account and itinerary. Stop before checkout; if the final discount cannot be observed safely, request redacted manual evidence and label any estimate conditional.

## Delta

[TakeOff 15 terms](https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/airline-credit-cards/takeoff-15) currently include eligible primary Delta SkyMiles Gold, Platinum and Reserve cards and their eligible business variants. The mileage discount is at least 15%; additional cardholders alone are not eligible. Check the linked SkyMiles account and an entirely Delta/Delta Connection-operated and ticketed award booked through Delta. Partner-operated awards and Pay with Miles/Miles + Cash are excluded. Taxes/fees remain payable, with the eligible-card payment requirement checked separately. Do not confuse Delta Gold with the non-Delta Amex Gold card, or infer lounge access from this award benefit.

## United

[United’s program announcement](https://united.mediaroom.com/2026-02-19-United-Gives-MileagePlus-Credit-Cardholders-More-Miles,-Exclusive-Flight-Discounts) describes at least 10% fewer miles for eligible cardholders, or at least 15% with Premier status, for the April 2026 program change. Verify the exact current card terms, for example [Club](https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/club-infinite) or [Quest](https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/united-quest), the primary MileagePlus account and eligible United/United Express award. Do not extend this to partner-operated awards, every card product or Money + Miles. Expanded Saver inventory, percentage pricing and issued fixed-mile discounts are distinct benefits: verify availability, expiry and stacking rather than adding them all automatically.

## Comparable value

Record gross comparable cash total, final miles required after any evidenced discount, mandatory award taxes/carrier surcharges/fees in the same currency, discount evidence and observation time. Keep any optional extra or card statement credit in a separate consistent scenario. A mileage discount changes the denominator; award cash charges reduce the numerator. Cash-price taxes already included in the comparable cash total are not subtracted a second time.

Synthetic example: USD 900 cash versus 50,000 miles + USD 100 = 1.6 US cents/mile. If a stipulated eligible 15% mileage discount produces an observed 42,500-mile quote with the same USD 100 charges, value is `(900 − 100) / 42,500 × 100`, about 1.882 US cents/mile. Use 42,500 once, not another 15% reduction; never discount the USD 100 without separate evidence. Real quotes may use provider-specific rounding or greater savings, so use displayed final miles rather than computing an exact live price from “at least” terms.
