# Hilton Aspire and Surpass checks

These source routes were reviewed on 2026-10-17 for **U.S. American Express** products. Confirm the user's issuing market and fetch current terms for every trip. Amounts, benefit periods, exclusions and product names can change; do not turn this guide into a standing rate table. Public terms do not verify a user's remaining allowance or certificate inventory. Amex pages can require a browser or login; use user-supplied redacted terms when blocked.

## Aspire

Start with [Aspire benefits](https://global.americanexpress.com/card-benefits/view-all/hilton-aspire) and [detailed Aspire terms](https://global.americanexpress.com/card-benefits/terms/hilton-aspire).

- Resort credit: check the actual property against the [participating resort list](https://www.hilton.com/en/aspireresortcredit/), current half-year allowance and remaining balance, direct merchant/payment requirements, excluded advance-purchase/nonrefundable rates and room-billed incidentals. A Hilton brand or resort fee alone does not prove participation; a portal room charge is not a direct resort charge.
- Flight credit: verify current quarter, eligible airfare/channel, payment card and transaction exclusions. Do not confuse this with another card's selected-airline incidental-fee credit, or assume award taxes, travel-bank funds or gift cards qualify.
- Free-night rewards: distinguish issued annual rewards from additional spend-triggered rewards. Verify issuance and expiry rather than assuming possession because the user holds Aspire.
- Hilton status: verify current card-conferred status in the linked loyalty account and property/brand/region rules. Do not promise an upgrade, universal breakfast or additional benefits on an ineligible booking channel.
- Aspire property-credit rate: inspect the special booking route/rate, eligible Waldorf Astoria/Conrad property, minimum stay and qualifying on-property spending. Compare its full price with other rates. This property credit is separate from the resort statement credit; verify stacking and never treat it as a room-rate refund.

## Surpass

Start with [Surpass benefits](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/account/get-started/hiltonsurpass/uncover-your-benefits) and [Hilton credit terms](https://global.americanexpress.com/card-benefits/detail/hilton-credit/hilton-surpass).

- Hilton credit: check current calendar-quarter cap and unused amount, direct Hilton reservation/merchant requirements, eligible room-billed incidentals and shared allowance for additional cards. Do not substitute the annual headline amount or apply Aspire's resort-only test to Surpass.
- Free-night reward: check whether the required eligible spending has been met **and the reward issued**. Card ownership alone does not supply an annual certificate. Do not propose extra spending just to make an assumed reward appear in this itinerary.
- Status: distinguish card-provided Gold from any separately earned/upgraded status and verify it in the loyalty account. Do not copy Aspire's status or resort/flight credits onto Surpass.

## Match Hilton benefits to the stay

Use [free-night reward terms](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/free-night-reward-terms/), [Honors terms](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/terms/) and [member benefits](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/member-benefits/). Verify standard-room availability, property exclusions, occupancy, certificate stay-by expiry, remaining fees and cancellation/redeposit rules. If redemption requires a phone call, give the user the official route; do not call or redeem for them, or collect the redemption code.

Distinguish a certificate from Hilton's fifth-night-free pricing on eligible all-points standard-room stays. Do not assume four points nights plus a certificate earns another free night. Inspect the quoted multi-night total instead of subtracting a night again. Verify breakfast versus daily food-and-beverage treatment by brand/region, guest count and eligible spend; separate it from card statement credits and any already-included meals. Provider research supplies the exact room, channel and terms before the benefit ledger can rank the result.
