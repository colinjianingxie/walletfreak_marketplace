# Other hotel and flight cards

Use the exact product, issuing market, personal/business variant and current issuer benefit guide. The routes below are starting points reviewed on 2026-10-17, not an exhaustive card catalog or a promise of benefits. For an unlisted card, find its official issuer guide and use the same ledger; label unresolved product details. Do not apply U.S. terms to another market or assume every card from one issuer has the same benefits.

For airline companion certificates or fare discounts, verify issued inventory, eligible routes/cabins/fare classes, seats for both travelers, booking/travel deadlines, blackout rules, required taxes/fees and payment restrictions. A companion benefit does not mean a free second ticket on every route. For cardholder award discounts or expanded availability, inspect the signed-in quote; do not apply a discount again to a price that already includes it.

Use the itinerary's actual baggage needs. A second source of the same free checked bag is not an extra saving; a partner-operated flight is not automatically covered by the marketing airline's card. Check whether the eligible cardholder is traveling and whether additional-card holders qualify independently. Compare any required payment card with the one needed for a credit or travel protection, rather than assuming both can apply to a single payment.

Lounge access needs the airport/terminal, operating hours, boarding-pass/carrier rules, guest count, enrollment, visit allowance and capacity restrictions. Travel insurance/protections need the current guide's covered traveler, eligible payment, award-tax/split-payment rules, exclusions and coverage conditions. Report these as conditional protections, never a guaranteed payout or a discount from the fare. Users perform all enrollments, account linking and transactions themselves.
