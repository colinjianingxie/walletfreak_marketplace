# Official lounge sources

Source routes reviewed 2026-10-17. Re-open current terms for every trip; this is not a permanent table of free visits, guest prices or card eligibility. An expired issuer partnership or older card version must not confer current access. Some pages require browser rendering or user-handled sign-in; unreadable terms remain unverified.

| Program / issuer | Official source route | Verify for this trip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amex Global Lounge Collection / Centurion | [Card-specific lounge finder](https://travelloungefinder.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/lounges/the-platinum-card), [U.S. Platinum terms](https://global.americanexpress.com/card-benefits/detail/the-lounge-collection/platinum) | Exact card/country, guest entitlement, spend-based unlock, entry window and partner-specific rules; do not transfer Platinum rules to Gold or Delta-branded cards |
| Priority Pass | [Entry credentials and lounge lookup](https://www.prioritypass.com/en-GB/accessing-our-airport-lounges) | Issuing card’s membership, activation, valid credential, specific lounge conditions, guest charges and excluded restaurant/experience benefits |
| Chase Sapphire | [Official lounge network and benefit terms](https://www.chase.com/sapphire-cards/lounges) | Reserve versus Preferred, personal versus business/additional-card rules, Chase-issued versus other Priority Pass access, participating partner locations |
| Capital One | [Lounge access guide](https://capitalonetravel.com/airport-lounges/lounge-access-guide) | Venture X versus other products, business/additional-card differences, effective dates, guest fees and spend-based guest eligibility |
| Delta Sky Club | [Access and policies](https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access) | Eligible card, visit allowance and its time-window definition, guest passes, remaining visits, carrier/fare exclusions; TakeOff 15 does not itself grant club access |
| United Club | [Access policies](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/airport/lounge-access.html), [Club card terms](https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/club-infinite) | Membership type versus one-time pass, guests, cardholder presence, same-day carrier, excluded lounges and capacity restrictions |

For another issuer/card, locate its official current benefits guide for the issuing country, then the lounge operator’s location and access rules. For every airport, use its official terminal/security map as well as the operator’s listing. Check departure versus transit/arrival access, airside connectivity, reopening/closure dates and local opening hours. If official pages disagree, label the conflict and ask for current user-visible evidence; do not pick the most generous rule.
