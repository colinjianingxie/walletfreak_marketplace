# Hilton: official sources and search guide

Programs/search aliases: Hilton Honors.

These are official entry points, not inventory or durable rate/benefit claims. Follow the current visible booking and loyalty navigation if a deep link moves. Verify the destination domain before a user signs in. Recheck names, terms and program participation during research.

- [Hilton booking](https://www.hilton.com/)
- [Hilton Honors](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/)

## Search steps and restrictions to verify

1. Select Use Points and distinguish Standard Room Rewards from Premium Room Rewards before comparing cash rates.
2. Check any multi-night award reduction against the traveler’s actual status and dates; inspect Points & Money separately.
3. Verify certificate usability, resort fees and breakfast or food-and-beverage benefits for the property and region.
4. Open the selected rate/fare terms before any checkout. Record the shared result fields in this skill, exact search parameters and observation time. If login is needed, let the user handle it. If blocked, use clearly labeled manually supplied results.

For every room compare the same dates, number of rooms, adults/children, bed type, meals, cancellation deadline and payment timing. Show total stay cost and mandatory charges payable at the property, even when excluded from the initial displayed total.

For Hilton Aspire or Surpass, use the bundled `card-travel-benefits` skill. Distinguish resort-only versus broader Hilton credits, issued certificates versus spend-triggered rewards, exact status, direct versus portal charges and eligible payment cards. Supply the specific property/rate and full quoted stay total; do not assume a card headline applies to this stay.
