# Trip intake

Accept ordinary language or the local dashboard brief. Reuse supplied facts before asking one grouped follow-up for essential gaps. Ask for origin, destinations/alternatives, dates or travel windows, duration, flexibility, travelers, rooms, budget/currency/basis and key preferences. Children's ages are needed only for relevant fares or occupancy. Never require a complete wallet or account login.

Distinguish fixed departure/return with ± days, a departure window plus duration, and a window within which the entire trip must fit. Do not reinterpret one as another. Ask about contradictory dates or durations. For undecided dates, continue destination/season research with stated assumptions rather than fabricating an itinerary.

Optional preferences: cabin, stops, arrival/departure times, baggage, refundable fares/rates, hotel location, beds, accessibility needs and priorities. Optional wallet facts: exact card products/market, points by program/owner, status, issued certificates, remaining credits and points to preserve. No passwords, codes, account/card numbers or passport details.

Show a compact trip brief before live searches. The user’s conversation request authorizes the scoped research; collect missing essentials there. The results viewer has no intake form or research controls. Label optional assumptions. If dates or scope change, state the revised scope in the conversation and clearly label the dates of prior findings.

Example: "NYC to Tokyo/Kyoto for two adults in April, 7–10 nights, departure flexible by three days. Economy, one stop maximum, near transit. USD 4,000 total; compare cash and Chase/Amex points. Show my local trip board."
